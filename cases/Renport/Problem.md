# Case 01: Fleet & Fulfillment System Failure

## Context

Renport Logistics operates across East Africa and Canada, managing corporate deliveries, e-commerce orders, and housing-related logistics. The system relies on coordination between fleet availability, routing, order approvals, and customer communication.  

As demand increased, operational strain became evident:
- Rising failed and delayed deliveries
- Slow new order approvals
- Increasing cancellations before fulfillment
- Revenue outcomes not matching reported KPIs

Standard dashboards and metrics failed to expose the **systemic bottlenecks**, leaving leadership with incomplete visibility.
