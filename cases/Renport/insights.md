
## Key Insights

- Fleet delays as a hidden bottleneck:** Fleets with the highest average fulfillment delay drove order approval backlogs and increased cancellations.  
- Cascading operational effects:** Delays in fulfillment directly suppressed realized revenue and distorted reported KPIs.  
- Data gaps exposed systemic risk:** Dashboards tracked attempts and completions but missed cancellations and delayed approvals, masking true performance.  
- Route and approval alignment:** Certain routes consistently caused delays, indicating misallocation of fleet resources.  
- Operational feedback loops:** High delays in one month predicted future cancellations and slower approvals in subsequent months, highlighting systemic risk patterns.
