# Executive Recommendation – Fintech Transaction Volume Decline

## Findings Summary

After analyzing transaction and CRM data, we identified that:

* Engagement among existing users remained stable.
* User mix shifts were minimal and did not explain the decline.
* Operational friction was negligible with failure rates under 0.5%.
* **Primary driver:** Pricing increases on select high-volume products caused a measurable reduction in transaction counts, especially among price-sensitive users.

## Recommendation

1. **Review Pricing Strategy:**

   * Adjust fees on affected products or introduce tiered pricing to retain high-value users.
   * Conduct controlled A/B testing to measure volume response to fee changes.

2. **Targeted Communication:**

   * Notify impacted users about fee adjustments or promotions to maintain engagement and confidence.

3. **Monitor Metrics Closely:**

   * Track transaction counts and revenue per product weekly.
   * Perform cohort analysis to ensure volume stabilizes post-adjustment.
