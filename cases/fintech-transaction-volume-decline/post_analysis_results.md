## Post-Implementation Results

- Within 3 weeks of adjusting fees, transaction volume recovered by approximately **6%**, validating the hypothesis that pricing was the primary driver.  
- High-value, price-sensitive users returned to previous activity levels.  
- Overall revenue remained stable due to a combination of volume recovery and slightly higher average transaction fees on unaffected products.  
- Weekly monitoring dashboards now include fee-impact tracking for proactive management.
