## Key Insights

- **High engagement did not equal revenue:** Clicks and impressions overestimated the actual revenue impact of campaigns.  
- **Operational bottlenecks suppressed conversion:** Delays in fleet fulfillment and order approval prevented high-engagement leads from converting.  
- **Reputational events impacted conversion:** Negative influencer events or regional reputational issues reduced realized revenue despite strong engagement.  
- **Misaligned KPI tracking:** Standard marketing KPIs failed to account for operational constraints and external risks, creating misleading dashboards.
