# Case 02: Marketing Signals vs Revenue Reality

## Context

Renport Logistics ran influencer and digital marketing campaigns across multiple regions to drive corporate and e-commerce order volume. Campaigns showed **high engagement metrics** (click-through rates, impressions, social interactions), but **revenue from campaigns consistently lagged** behind expectations.  

Standard marketing dashboards failed to integrate **operational constraints**, leaving leadership unable to explain the disconnect between digital engagement and realized revenue.
