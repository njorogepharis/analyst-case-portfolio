## Decisions Enabled

- Resource optimization: Reallocated fleets to high-impact routes and shifted schedules to minimize delays.  
- Workflow redesign: Adjusted approval processes to reduce cascading cancellations and better align with fleet capacity.  
- Integrated performance monitoring: Combined operational, fleet, and customer metrics into unified dashboards to provide leadership with real-time visibility of bottlenecks.  
- Proactive revenue protection: Identified high-risk orders likely to fail or cancel, enabling preemptive intervention.  
- Strategic planning: Provided insight into capacity expansion and demand management, linking operational performance directly to revenue outcomes.
