## Case Structure

Each case follows a consistent structure to highlight senior-level analytical thinking:

1. **Context** — Understanding the system, environment, and stakeholders.  
2. **Executive Problem** — Defining the challenge that leadership faced or could not explain.  
3. **Analytical Approach** — Detailing the reasoning, methodology, and problem-solving process, with emphasis on insight over tools.  
4. **Key Insights** — Highlighting systemic findings and root causes discovered through analysis.  
5. **Decisions Enabled** — Translating insights into actionable guidance for leadership or operational change.

This structure ensures that each case demonstrates **problem-solving, systems-level understanding, and strategic impact**, rather than simply technical execution.

## Cases

### 1. Fintech — Revenue & Risk Analysis
**Context:**  
Analyzed payment systems, transaction flows, and customer behavior across multiple fintech platforms to detect revenue leakage and operational risk.  

**Executive Problem:**  
Leadership observed declining net revenue and inconsistent transaction approvals, despite stable customer activity metrics.  

**Analytical Approach:**  
- Mapped transaction lifecycle from initiation to settlement.  
- Isolated points of operational failure causing revenue leakage.  
- Applied pattern detection and anomaly analysis to highlight systemic inefficiencies.  

**Key Insights:**  
- Identified bottlenecks causing delayed settlements.  
- Found gaps in fraud detection processes, leading to unrecognized chargebacks.  
- Mismatched customer journey tracking created misleading KPIs.  

**Decisions Enabled:**  
- Optimized payment routing and approval workflows.  
- Strengthened fraud detection logic, reducing losses.  
- Aligned KPI tracking with real revenue impact.

### 2. Logistics — Renport: Fleet & Fulfillment System Failure
**Context:**  
Analyzed Renport Logistics operations across East Africa and Canada, managing corporate, e-commerce, and housing deliveries.  

**Executive Problem:**  
Rising delivery delays, failed orders, and slow new order approvals were impacting revenue and customer trust, with no single point of failure identified.  

**Analytical Approach:**  
- Mapped the end-to-end order lifecycle: attempted, approved, fulfilled, and failed orders.  
- Identified feedback loops where fleet delays suppressed order approvals and triggered cancellations.  
- Analyzed timing dependencies across fleet capacity, routing, and operational workflows.  

**Key Insights:**  
- Fleet delays acted as a hidden upstream bottleneck affecting revenue realization.  
- Slow approvals and cancellations distorted reported demand and capacity metrics.  
- Operational bottlenecks amplified systemic risk across marketing and fulfillment.  

**Decisions Enabled:**  
- Reallocated fleet resources to high-impact routes.  
- Adjusted order approval workflows to reduce cascading cancellations.  
- Aligned operational capacity with demand planning and marketing forecasts.

### 3. Marketing & Revenue Systems — Campaign Performance Analysis
**Context:**  
Analyzed the performance of influencer and digital campaigns across multiple regions and platforms.  

**Executive Problem:**  
Despite high engagement metrics (clicks, impressions), revenue from campaigns declined sharply, and conversion KPIs did not reflect actual user behavior.  

**Analytical Approach:**  
- Integrated marketing analytics with operational order data.  
- Identified external reputational risks and operational constraints affecting conversion.  
- Investigated attribution models to reveal blind spots in campaign reporting.  

**Key Insights:**  
- Campaign engagement was decoupled from revenue due to fulfillment bottlenecks.  
- External reputational events impacted conversion but were invisible in standard dashboards.  
- Operational friction caused low realized conversions despite strong digital metrics.  

**Decisions Enabled:**  
- Adjusted marketing spend and channel strategy to align with operational capacity.  
- Incorporated external sentiment tracking into campaign analysis.  
- Improved cross-functional visibility between marketing and operations for real-time KPI accuracy.

### 4. Operations & Strategy — Process Optimization & Decision Workflows
**Context:**  
Analyzed operational processes across multiple industries to identify workflow inefficiencies and decision-making bottlenecks.  

**Executive Problem:**  
Leadership faced delays in approvals, escalations, and decision-making that impacted revenue, delivery, and risk management.  

**Analytical Approach:**  
- Conducted end-to-end process mapping across departments.  
- Identified decision bottlenecks and process redundancies.  
- Applied data-driven simulations to test workflow improvements.  

**Key Insights:**  
- Redundant approvals slowed operational throughput.  
- Key decisions were delayed due to misaligned cross-department responsibilities.  
- Information silos created hidden risks in both operational and financial workflows.  

**Decisions Enabled:**  
- Streamlined approval processes to reduce delays.  
- Redesigned decision workflows for higher operational efficiency.  
- Established cross-functional dashboards for proactive management.
